Preface

Big Local News has partnered with the Deportation Data Project to provide local reporters with simplified datasets and story recipes that can help them mine the millions of records of people caught in the immigration enforcement system.

BLN has enhanced some of the data to improve its usefulness for local reporters attempting specific stories, which are documented in this guide. But every story idea can’t be boiled down to simple instructions. We encourage you to contact us for help when you have a story idea that might come from these records.

Organization

Part I of this guide introduces you to the data sources, alternatives, and stories that have already been done with deportation data. It includes a broad overview of the data system, including what it can and can’t tell us.

Part II provides recipes for specific stories like the ones highlighted in Part I. For each of the main data tables, it provides an introduction on how to obtain just the information for your area. Each subsequent chapter provides a short recipe for doing the analysis for a single story.

The Appendices provide additional resources, including full documentation of the data provide by BLN and some background on ICE and immigration legal terms.
